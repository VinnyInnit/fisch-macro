FISCH MACRO - zero setup
========================

OPEN IT
  Mac:     right-click "Start Fisch Macro (Mac).command" > Open > Open.
           (Every newly downloaded copy must be opened this way once. Double-clicking
           a fresh download does nothing visible because macOS blocks it.)
  Windows: double-click "Start Fisch Macro (Windows).bat"
           (SmartScreen: More info > Run anyway.)

  First run installs what it needs (internet, about a minute), then the Fisch
  Macro window opens in your browser. Keep the small Terminal window open.

UPDATING FROM AN OLDER VERSION
  Close the old Terminal window first (Cmd+Q on Terminal), then open the new
  launcher the same way. The first start installs the fast screen reader (a few
  seconds). Old saved settings are cleaned up automatically.

MAC PERMISSIONS (one time) - WITHOUT THESE IT CAN'T WORK
  System Settings > Privacy & Security, switch "Terminal" ON under:
    - Accessibility      (presses the mouse/keys - without it the bar never moves)
    - Screen Recording   (sees the game)
    - Input Monitoring   (F6 pause key)
  Then quit Terminal (Cmd+Q) and open the launcher again. The window shows a
  yellow warning while any of these is off.

GAME SETTINGS (one time)
  - Nothing needed for shaking: on Mac the macro reads the screen for the Shake
    button and clicks it only while one is showing. With no button it presses
    nothing, so it can't open your inventory or the menus.
  - Fisch settings: Higher Brightness, Higher Saturation and Wind Shake OFF.

USE IT
  Equip your rod, press Start, click into Roblox. That's it. The macro:
    - finds the Roblox window and casts from the middle of the game view
    - reads your rod, its enchant and your area from the screen (Mac) and looks
      them up in Fisch Wiki data (Control = bar size, Resilience, special minigames)
    - finds the reel bar on screen by itself at the first bite, and learns what
      your minigame looks like (works with most rod skins too)
    - only counts the reel as up once it really sees the track (edges, ends) and it
      moves - so it shakes properly and never "reels" a still picture
    - keeps the fish in the bar, reels, and repeats
    - casts again if no reel shows up within 25 s (change "Cast again if no reel
      after" in Settings)
    - reads the screen up to 60 times a second on Mac (ScreenCaptureKit)
  Pause: F6 (fn+F6 on a MacBook) or move the mouse into the top-left corner.

  Only if it can't find the reel: open "Manual setup" in the window.

SPECIAL RODS
  - Pinion's Aria: detected automatically; the macro lands the falling notes in
    your bar (that's what makes it a perfect catch) and follows the bird between notes.
  - Remembrance: detected automatically; white fish in the bar, dark fish out.
  - Other note rods (Astraeus Serenade, Noiseform, Santa's Miracle, Tranquility):
    the fish is kept in the bar, notes aren't chased yet.
  - Rods with two fish (D.R.E.D., Eldritch, Splitbranch Twig): it follows one.
  - Windows: no rod/enchant/area reading and no Shake-button reading.

Heads-up: macros are usually against Fisch's and Roblox's rules. Use at your own risk.
